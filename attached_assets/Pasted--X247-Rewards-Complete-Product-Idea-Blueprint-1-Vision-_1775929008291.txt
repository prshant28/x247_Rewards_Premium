# X247 Rewards — Complete Product Idea Blueprint

## 1) Vision

**X247 Rewards** is a premium referral-driven reward platform designed to convert Google event registrations into a gamified community growth engine.

The platform helps users:

* Register through **two strategic referral links**
* Complete a **verification Google Form**
* Join a **private WhatsApp updates community**
* Unlock **daily swag rewards, vouchers, hackathons, and exclusive opportunities**
* Track progress through a **live referral dashboard**

The core idea is to combine **Google community event participation + referrals + rewards + exclusivity** into a high-conversion landing experience.

---

## 2) Core User Journey

### Step 1: Register

Users land on the website and register through:

* **Referral Link 1 → Google Solution Challenge / Google Event**
* **Referral Link 2 → Bonus registration link**

Completing both increases their reward eligibility.

### Step 2: Verify

After registration, users must submit a **Google Form** with:

* Full name
* Email
* WhatsApp number
* Unique referral code
* Screenshot / proof of registration (optional)

### Step 3: Community Access

After successful verification, users are invited to:

* Private WhatsApp updates group
* Hackathon announcements
* Future Google community contests
* Voucher drops
* Swag campaigns

### Step 4: Rewards

Users unlock:

* **21 daily winners → $50 swag rewards**
* **Every 10 referrals → $50 gift card**
* **50 verified referrals → $99 Gen AI Leader voucher**
* **Exclusive invite-only hackathon access (₹499 equivalent free)**
